Device information check

This page can share basic browser/device details and battery status with the site administrator.

What may be collected: device type, browser, available OS/device hints, screen size, language, timezone and battery level/charging state where the browser supports it. Your IP address is received by the server.

Battery and device model details are not available in every browser. This page does not request GPS location, contacts, photos, IMEI or phone number.

Only submit if you understand and agree to this collection.
